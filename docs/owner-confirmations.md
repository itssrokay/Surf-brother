# Owner confirmation checklist

These are questions to settle before a public launch, not reasons to withhold this working private proposal.

1. **Stay & Surf price basis:** per person, per bed, per booking or another unit? Does each supplied rate cover the same unit?
2. **Savings basis:** is ₹1,000 off per person or booking? Confirm eligibility, availability, any dates/exclusions, and whether the advance amount is affected. None of those missing terms was invented.
3. **Meals:** number of meals, meal-day count for each duration, price unit, vegetarian/non-vegetarian options and dietary handling.
4. **Where lessons happen:** actual surf beach/meeting point, how weather changes the location, group lesson timings, transfers and any costs. The map on the site locates the stay.
5. **Stay allocation:** which photographs show AC vs Non-AC; sharing arrangement, room/bed counts, occupancy policies and whether any private-room option exists. No private-room or mixed-dorm claim is made.
6. **Bathroom coverage:** current complete bathroom photos, amenities and sharing setup. One room photograph includes a washbasin; that does not establish a bathroom specification.
7. **Camping:** what tent, bedding and other equipment is provided, bathroom access and weather arrangements.
8. **Coaching:** current named instructors, verified qualifications, experience and languages; exact group size; swimming/age/health participation requirements and safety arrangements.
9. **Private 1:1:** session duration, equipment and exact payment/cancellation applicability.
10. **Activities:** scheduling/frequency for included social events; currently available paid kayaking or other extras, pricing and conditions. Bioluminescence remains a natural phenomenon, never guaranteed.
11. **Practical arrival details:** arrival/departure times, last-mile travel, transfer options/costs, Wi-Fi suitability and any relevant house rules. None is assumed.
12. **Media and launch approval:** permission for public use of all owner photos/video, identifiable guests and credited review excerpts; photographer credits; approval of the new typographic brand treatment and conceptual board artwork.
13. **Launch details:** approved final copy/rates, chosen domain and hosting, ownership of those accounts, privacy/analytics requirements and an agreed maintenance scope. Public publishing is outside this request.

Until settled, the prototype shows listed rates, exact nights and transparent inclusions, builds an enquiry, and asks the team to confirm totals and availability. It never reserves beds, applies a guaranteed discount or takes payment.
