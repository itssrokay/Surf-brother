# A website proposal for SurfBrothers Mulki

**Stay • Surf • Belong — with a clearer path from curiosity to enquiry.**

Your current site makes it easy to find a phone number. This proposal helps a visitor understand the whole SurfBrothers experience before they get in touch: the lessons, the real stay, the people and the practical details.

The new design uses your actual photographs and evening footage, with an original branded surfboard artwork created in Blender. It brings your identity into a memorable coastal visual system that works on a phone as well as a laptop.

An original scroll-controlled Blender scene carries visitors from a board on the shore to paddling out and riding a wave. Gentle motion across the rest of the page supports that story. Visitors can skip straight to lessons or packages, and those who prefer reduced motion get a static version. The illustrated scene expresses the surf experience without claiming to depict your actual beach.

Visitors can compare Surf Only and Stay & Surf, choose course length and accommodation, see the exact listed rate and nights, and understand meals and payment terms. Their selections become a WhatsApp message they can review before sending. That gives your team the details needed to answer an enquiry without suggesting that a bed has already been reserved.

The gallery, beginner course comparison, sample day, guest stories, directions and practical FAQs answer common questions in one place. Unknown price units, room details and transfer arrangements remain questions for your team, rather than misleading assumptions.

## What I'd hand over

- The complete responsive website source and production build, with a single editable business-content file.
- Your optimised photographs and silent video, retained originals and an asset-rights manifest.
- Both editable Blender projects, generation scripts, original renders, the animated scene and responsive web exports.
- Clear editing/run instructions, test results and a short list of business facts to confirm before launch.
- Desktop/mobile screenshots and an initial walkthrough so you can review everything comfortably.

No booking system subscription or backend is needed for this version. Domain, hosting and any later services would be chosen and agreed separately; nothing has been bought or published.

## Proposed exchange

I'd propose handing over this website, incorporating one consolidated round of your factual/content feedback, and helping prepare it for an agreed launch in exchange for **one 5-day / 4-night Non-AC Stay & Surf trip for one guest**. If a full exchange doesn't suit, we could agree a discount toward that trip instead, or discuss a cash purchase of the same handover.

Meals and any extras would be agreed separately. We'd confirm the package's price basis, dates, availability and the exchange terms together before committing. Ongoing maintenance and any additional features would have a separate agreed scope.

This is a concrete website proposal, with no promise about booking volume or revenue. The next step is simply to review the private local version, confirm the outstanding details and decide whether the exchange works for both sides.
